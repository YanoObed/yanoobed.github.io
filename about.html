About Me

I’m Obadia Yano, a Data Analytics Software Developer working at the intersection of data analytics, software development, application development, and data engineering.

My work focuses on building practical data-driven solutions that transform raw data into reliable information, analytical products, dashboards, applications, and decision-support systems.
